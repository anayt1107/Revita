# Congressional App Challenge

The Congressional App Challenge is a nationwide competition aimed at encouraging students to engage with computer science and technology through the creation of their own apps. Our project, Revita, aims to promote community-driven environmental action among urban college students.

## Target Audience
Our primary target audience includes urban college students who are eager to participate in environmental initiatives.

## ResNet AI Model
Revita incorporates a ResNet AI model that has achieved an impressive accuracy of 96% by training on diverse datasets provided by Kaggle. This high level of precision enables our application to deliver reliable information and suggestions to users regarding their environmental impact.

## Gamification Features
To enhance user engagement, Revita implements several gamification features:
- **Leaderboard:** Users can see how they rank concerning their engagement in environmental actions.
- **Leveling System:** As users participate more, they can level up, unlocking new features and rewards.
- **Achievements:** Users can earn badges for completing specific tasks or reaching milestones.
- **Friending:** Users can connect with friends, encouraging a community approach to sustainability.

## Technology Stack
- **Frontend:** Developed using React Native, providing a smooth and interactive user experience.
- **Backend:** Built with Python and TensorFlow, ensuring robust performance and machine learning capabilities.

## Vision
Our vision is to foster a community-driven approach to environmental action, empowering urban college students to make informed decisions that positively impact our environment through the use of technology. Revita stands as a testament to the innovative spirit of the Congressional App Challenge and the potential for technology to drive social change.
